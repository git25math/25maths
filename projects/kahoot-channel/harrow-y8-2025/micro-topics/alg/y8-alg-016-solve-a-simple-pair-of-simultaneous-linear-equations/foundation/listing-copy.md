## Kahoot Description (EN)
Year 8 Foundation practice aligned to Y8-ALG-016. Objective: Solve a simple pair of simultaneous linear equations by visual observation or by eliminating one variable. This can be introduced using real-life examples (2 bags + 1 hat = 23 and 3 bags and 1 hat = 33 or picture magic squares). Includes 12 exam-style MCQs. 对应学习目标：通过视觉观察或消元法解决简单的二元一次方程组。这可以通过现实生活中的例子引入（如2袋子+1帽子=23，3袋子1帽子=33或图神器方格）。。共12道选择题。
