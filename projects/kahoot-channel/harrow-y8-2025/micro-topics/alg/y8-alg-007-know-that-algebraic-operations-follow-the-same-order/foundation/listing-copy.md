Year 8 Foundation practice aligned to Y8-ALG-007. Objective: Know that algebraic operations follow the same order as arithmetic operations when substituting positive and negative integers into simple linear expressions/formulae and substitute positive and negative integers into formulae, linear expressions and expressions involving small powers, including examples that lead to an equation to solve. Includes 12 exam-style MCQs. 对应学习目标：了解代数运算遵循与算术运算相同的顺序，将正负整数代入简单的线性表达式/公式，并代入涉及低次幂的公式，包括解方程的示例。。共12道选择题。
