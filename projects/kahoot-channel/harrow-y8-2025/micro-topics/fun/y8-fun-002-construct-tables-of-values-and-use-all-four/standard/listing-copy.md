## Kahoot Description (EN)
Year 8 Standard practice aligned to Y8-FUN-002. Objective: Construct tables of values and use all four quadrants to plot the graphs of linear functions, where y is given explicitly in terms of x; recognise that equations of the form y = mx + c correspond to straight-line graphs. Includes 15 exam-style MCQs. 对应学习目标：构建数值表，并在所有四个象限中绘制线性函数的图表，其中y是以x形式表示；识别y=mx+c形式的方程对应线性图。。共15道选择题。
