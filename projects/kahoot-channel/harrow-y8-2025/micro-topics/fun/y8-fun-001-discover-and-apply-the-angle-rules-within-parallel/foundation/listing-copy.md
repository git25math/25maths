## Kahoot Description (EN)
Year 8 Foundation practice aligned to Y8-FUN-001. Objective: Discover and apply the angle rules within parallel lines (Alternate, Corresponding, Co-interior, vertically opposite) and apply these rules in a range of geometrical problems. Includes 12 exam-style MCQs. 对应学习目标：发现并应用平行线内的角度规则（内错角、同位角、同旁内角、对顶角），并在一系列几何问题中应用这些规则。。共12道选择题。
