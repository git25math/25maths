# Year 8 | Y8-TRI-002 | Calculate the sum of angles at a point | Standard

## Learning Objective
Calculate the sum of angles at a point, on a straight line and in a triangle: Prove that vertically opposite angles are equal, Derive and use the property that the angle sum of a quadrilateral is 360 degrees.
计算点、直线上及三角形内的角度和：证明对顶角相等；推导并使用四边形的内角和为360度的性质。
