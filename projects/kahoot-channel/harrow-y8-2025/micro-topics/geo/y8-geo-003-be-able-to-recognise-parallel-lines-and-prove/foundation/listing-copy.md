## Kahoot Description (EN)
Year 8 Foundation practice aligned to Y8-GEO-003. Objective: Be able to recognise parallel lines and prove this by the basic fact that if two straight lines are intersected by a third line, and if the opposite angles are equal, then the two lines are parallel. Includes 12 exam-style MCQs. 对应学习目标：能够识别平行线，并通过基本事实证明：如果两条直线被第三条线截断且内错角相等，那么这两条直线平行。。共12道选择题。
