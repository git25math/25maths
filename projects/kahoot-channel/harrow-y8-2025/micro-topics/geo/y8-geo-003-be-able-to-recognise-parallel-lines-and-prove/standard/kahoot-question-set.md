# Year 8 | Y8-GEO-003 | Be able to recognise parallel lines and prove | Standard

## Learning Objective
Be able to recognise parallel lines and prove this by the basic fact that if two straight lines are intersected by a third line, and if the opposite angles are equal, then the two lines are parallel.
能够识别平行线，并通过基本事实证明：如果两条直线被第三条线截断且内错角相等，那么这两条直线平行。
