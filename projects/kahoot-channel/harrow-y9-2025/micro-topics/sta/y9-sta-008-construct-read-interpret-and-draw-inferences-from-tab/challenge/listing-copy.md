## Kahoot Description (EN)
Year 9 Challenge practice aligned to Y9-STA-008. Objective: Construct, read, interpret, and draw inferences from tables and statistical data from bar charts, pie charts, pictograms, stem-and-leaf diagrams and frequency tables. This can consist of averages of given data. Includes 15 exam-style MCQs. 对应学习目标：构建、读取、解释和从表格和统计数据中得出推论，如条形图、饼图、象形图、茎叶图和频率表。这可以包括给定数据的平均值。。共15道选择题。
