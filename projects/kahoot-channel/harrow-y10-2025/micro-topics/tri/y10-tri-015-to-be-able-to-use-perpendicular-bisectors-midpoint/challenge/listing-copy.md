## Kahoot Description (EN)
Year 10 Challenge practice aligned to Y10-TRI-015. Objective: To be able to use perpendicular bisectors (midpoint joined to opposite vertex) to find the centre of gravity of a triangle (centroid). Understand the meaning of inscribed and circumscribed circles in relation to triangles and bisectors. Includes 15 exam-style MCQs. 对应学习目标：能够使用垂直平分线（中点连接到相对顶点）找到三角形的重心。理解内接圆和外接圆与三角形和平分线的关系。。共15道选择题。
