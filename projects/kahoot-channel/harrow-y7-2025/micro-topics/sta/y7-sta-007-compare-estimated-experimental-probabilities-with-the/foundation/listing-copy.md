## Kahoot Description (EN)
Year 7 Foundation practice aligned to Y7-STA-007. Objective: Compare estimated experimental probabilities with theoretical probabilities, recognising that when experiments are repeated different outcomes may result and increasing the number of times an experiment is repeated generally leads to better estimates of probability. Includes 12 exam-style MCQs. 对应学习目标：将估计的实验概率与理论概率进行比较，认识到重复实验可能会产生不同的结果，并且增加实验次数通常会使得概率估算的更准确。。共12道选择题。
